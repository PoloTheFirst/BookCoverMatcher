## 4. Phase 9 release gate 1 — Resolve `display-mask` licensing

This is a hard legal release gate.

The unresolved artifact is:

`com.microsoft.device.display:display-mask:0.3.0`

The previously identified dependency path is:

`MSAL → common → display-mask`

The artifact was reported to be packaged in the APK.

Previous investigation found:

- Its POM did not declare a licence.
- Its JAR was 1,591 bytes and contained no licence or notice file.
- Maven Central did not publish an artifact in that group.
- It was not found in the inspected Microsoft Surface Duo SDK repository.
- It was not found in the inspected MSAL common repository module list.

Reinvestigate using authoritative evidence.

### 4.1 Verify the licence

Look for:

- Actual artifact publisher.
- Official source repository.
- Authoritative upstream licence.
- Published source archive or source JAR.
- Official Microsoft documentation.
- Artifact copyright and notice information.

Do not infer the licence from Microsoft's general licensing practices or another dependency.

Do not relabel the artifact MIT or Apache-2.0 without evidence.

### 4.2 Consider removal only if technically safe

If no licence can be established, investigate whether the dependency can be excluded safely.

Before exclusion:

- Inspect runtime references.
- Establish why MSAL brings it in.
- Determine whether authentication or device compatibility depends on it.
- Add focused regression tests.
- Execute real-device sign-in, sign-out, and token-renewal verification.
- Verify the dependency is absent from the resulting APK.
- Record the exact exclusion and evidence.

Compilation alone does not prove safe removal.

Do not modify MSAL or introduce an unverified replacement merely to bypass the issue.

### 4.3 Stop condition

If the licence remains unresolved and safe exclusion cannot be demonstrated:

- Mark `display-mask` unresolved.
- Keep it as a hard stable-release blocker.
- Do not declare stable v2.6.0 licence-clear.
- Do not produce or distribute a stable release artifact.
- Do not falsely claim release readiness.

**This does not prevent publication of the development checkpoint tag.** The unresolved status must be documented in the report and development changelog.
